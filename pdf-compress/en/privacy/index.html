PDF Compressor Offline

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

PDF Compressor Offline is designed to reduce PDF file size directly on your device. This policy explains what data the app processes and how purchases are handled.

PrivacyTermsSupportApp InfoApple EULA

1. Offline PDF processing

PDF files are processed on your device. The app does not upload your PDFs to our servers for compression. The original file is not changed; a new compressed copy is created when possible.

2. Files and documents

Your selected PDFs are used only to perform the compression action you request. Compressed output is saved or shared only when you choose to do so. Compressed files are not kept by the app as a document library.

3. Information stored on device

The app may store basic local preferences and limited usage state needed for app functionality, such as free usage counters and purchase access state.

4. Purchases

Premium access is offered as a one-time lifetime purchase through Apple App Store. RevenueCat may be used to validate purchase status, restore purchases, and manage entitlement access.

5. Data we do not collect

We do not require an account, do not collect PDF content, do not collect precise location, and do not use advertising tracking for this app.

6. Support

For privacy questions or support, contact us at info@aristophia.com.
